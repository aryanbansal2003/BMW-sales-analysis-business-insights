This project presents an end-to-end data analysis of BMW sales data from 2010 to 2024. The objective is to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, customer preferences, pricing, and regional demand using Python-based data analysis techniques.

**Objectives**
Analyze sales trends over time,
Identify top-performing models and regions,
Understand the impact of price, mileage, and engine size on sales,
Discover high-potential segments for business growth,
Provide data-driven business recommendations.

**Tools & Technologies**
Python,
Pandas (Data Manipulation),
Matplotlib (Data Visualization),
Excel.

**Key Insights**
Sales trend is fluctuating, with peak performance in 2022,
Asia is the highest contributing region (~42.9M sales),
7 Series and X1 are among the top-performing models,
No strong correlation between price and sales volume,
Engine size does not directly determine pricing or demand.

**Analysis Performed**
Data cleaning and preprocessing,
Year-wise sales trend analysis,
Model-wise and region-wise performance,
Fuel type and transmission analysis,
Pricing, mileage, and engine size evaluation,
Sales classification (High vs Low),
Combination analysis (Model + Region, Fuel + Transmission).

**Business Recommendations**
Focus on high-performing models like 7 Series and X1,
Strengthen market presence in Asia,
Promote high-potential segments with targeted strategies,
Maintain a balance between premium and mid-range vehicles.

**Key Learnings**
Applied real-world data analytics techniques,
Improved skills in Python, data visualization, and business interpretation,
Learned how to convert raw data into actionable insights.
